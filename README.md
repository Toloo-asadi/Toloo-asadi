
# Toloo Asadi

MSc Digital Humanities student at the University of Genoa (expected March 2027), pursuing entry-level opportunities in Machine Learning and Computer Vision.

I work with Python to analyse human behaviour from video and motion data. My Master's thesis, in collaboration with researchers at the University of Genoa and IIT, investigates whether machine learning can distinguish four vitality forms: enthusiastic, fed up, gentle, and rude. The research is ongoing, and the dataset is not public.

## Technical Skills
- **Programming & Data:** Python, pandas
- **Machine Learning:** PyTorch, scikit-learn
- **Computer Vision:** OpenCV, facial landmarks, facial Action Units
- **Analysis:** Time-series analysis, Event Synchronization

## Selected Work
- **Smile Dynamics & Interpersonal Synchrony:** Python-based video analysis of facial expressions and interpersonal coordination.
- **Master's Thesis (in progress):** Computational analysis of human intentions through motion capture and video data.
